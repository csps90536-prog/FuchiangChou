關於我

自我介紹 / About Me

周福強博士現職長庚大學人工智慧中心博士級研究助理，專長為智慧醫療、訊號處理、人工智慧、自然語言處理等，曾任職飛利浦、宏達電、華為等公司，負責各種創新產品的研究與開發，也曾於銘傳大學、龍華科技大學擔任專任（案）教職，教授各式課程。周博士於1999年自台灣大學取得電機工程博士學位後，除了短期擔任教職外，主要於產業界服務，期間通過了有11個美國專利，與合計超過50個各國專利。並於宏達電任職期間，負責一個世界級醫療競賽（Tricorder），最後獲得亞軍以及100萬美元的獎金。綜觀周博士的經歷，除了具有相當的教學與研究能力外，也具有相當豐富的產業實務經驗。

Dr. Fu-Chiang Chou is currently a doctoral research assistant at the Center for Artificial Intelligence, Chang Gung University. His expertise includes smart healthcare, signal processing, artificial intelligence, and natural language processing. He has previously worked for companies such as Philips, HTC, and Huawei, where he was responsible for the research and development of various innovative products. He has also held full-time (or part-time) faculty positions at Ming Chuan University and Lung Hwa University of Science and Technology, teaching various courses. After obtaining his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from National Taiwan University in 1999, aside from short-term teaching positions, he primarily served in the industry, during which time he obtained 11 US patents and over 50 patents in various countries. During his time at HTC, he led a world-class medical competition (Tricorder), ultimately winning second place and a prize of US$1 million. Dr. Chou's experience demonstrates not only considerable teaching and research abilities but also extensive practical industry experience.
